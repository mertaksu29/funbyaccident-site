2. The Data We Collect

The data processed when you use our Service falls into three categories:

A. Data You Provide Directly to Us

  • In-Game Profile Information: The player nickname you choose and your selected in-game avatar.
  • Player Support Communications: Information and messages you provide when you contact our support team via email or submit feedback.

B. Gameplay Data Generated While Using the Service

  • Game Progress and Statistics: Your player level, unlocked heroes and upgrades, highest wave and survival time records, in-game gold balance, daily reward and energy status, and your game settings (language, control layout, audio, and notification preferences). Your gameplay progress is stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase Records: When you make an in-app purchase (such as the Ad-Free Bundle or Gold Packages), the store transaction identifier and entitlement status provided by the app store to verify and deliver your purchase. (We never collect or have access to your credit card or banking details).

C. Data Collected Automatically and Through Our Partners

For advertising, analytics, and security purposes, the following data may be collected automatically through our third-party service providers:

  • Device and Identifier Data: Device model, operating system and version, screen resolution, language settings, mobile advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID – GAID, Apple Identifier for Advertisers – IDFA), and app installation identifiers.
  • Coarse Location and Network Data: Approximate location derived from your IP address (country and city level only; we never collect precise GPS location) and network connection status.
  • Usage and Diagnostic Data: Interactions with in-game advertisements (ad views, clicks, and rewarded video completions), session duration, crash logs, and performance diagnostics.

3. Why We Collect Your Data

We use your data for the following legitimate purposes:

A. To Make the Service Work (Performance of a Contract)

  • To save your gameplay progress, hero upgrades, and settings so you can play the game,
  • To verify network connectivity and synchronize daily rewards and energy regeneration timers,
  • To verify and deliver your in-app purchases,
  • Where permitted by you, to schedule local reminder notifications on your device (such as when your energy is full or your daily free pack is ready).

B. To Improve and Secure the Service (Legitimate Interests)

  • To identify and fix bugs, crashes, and technical performance issues,
  • To protect the in-game economy and maintain fair play by preventing cheating, unauthorized modifications, and fraud,
  • To respond to your player support inquiries.

C. To Show Advertisements (Consent / Legitimate Interests)

  • To display optional rewarded advertisements (such as for energy refills, revives, or bonus rewards) and interstitial advertisements within the game,
  • To measure ad effectiveness and manage ad frequency.

6. Your Rights and Options

You have the following rights and choices regarding your data:

  • Opt Out of Personalized Advertising: You can limit ad tracking or reset your mobile advertising identifier at any time in your device settings:
    • Android: Open Settings > Google > Ads and select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
    • iOS: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and disable tracking permissions.
    • You can also purchase the in-game "Ad-Free Bundle" to disable forced interstitial advertisements.
  • Manage Notifications: You can disable reminder notifications at any time through your device's application notification settings.
  • Access, Correction, and Deletion of Your Data:
    • You can permanently delete your local gameplay progress and profile data at any time by using the in-game reset option, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the game.
    • To exercise your statutory privacy rights under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or KVKK (including requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data), please contact us at funbyaccidentgames@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. How We Protect Your Data and Data Retention

  • Security Safeguards: We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures—including encrypted network communications (HTTPS/TLS) and integrity verification—to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.
  • Data Retention: Gameplay data remains on your device for as long as the game is installed. Support emails and transaction records are retained only for as long as necessary to resolve your request and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Age Limits and Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), nor do we knowingly allow such persons to use our Service. If you are under the applicable age limit, please do not send any personal data about yourself to us. If you believe we might have any personal data from or about a child under the age limit, please contact us at funbyaccidentgames@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.
