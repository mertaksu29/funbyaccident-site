Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device, in the game's own storage. There is no account or cloud save, so progress does not move to another device. If your Apple device backup (iCloud or computer) includes app data, restoring the device may bring the progress back.

What happens if I delete the app?
Deleting the app also deletes the save file, and your progress is lost. We cannot restore it, because we never have a copy of your save.

How do I start over?
On the title screen tap “New Game”, then tap again to confirm. All progress is erased. (Sailing to a new harbor, the prestige feature, is different: it keeps your permanent bonuses.)

Are there purchases or ads?
No. The game is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

Do I need the internet?
No. The game works fully offline. Offline earnings and daily orders use your device clock.

How do I change the language?
Open the settings menu in the game (gear icon) and switch between Turkish and English.

What data do you collect?
None. See the Privacy Policy.
