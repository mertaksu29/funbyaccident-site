Privacy Policy

Fener Pazarı – Lantern Market (iOS)
Effective date: November 7, 2026

In short

Fener Pazarı – Lantern Market is a single-player game that works without an internet connection. The game does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting tools or in-app purchases.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (money, stalls, goals, etc.) and settings (language, music, sound) are kept only on your device, in a save file inside the app's own storage. The game reads your device clock and time zone to work out offline earnings and daily orders; this also stays on the device. None of this is sent to us or anyone else, and we cannot access it.

Deleting the app deletes the save file. You can also reset your progress in the game with “New Game”.

Permissions and network

The game does not connect to the internet and does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notification permission.

Apple

You download the app from the App Store. Apple may process data for its own services (for example download statistics, or crash reports if you turned on “Share with App Developers” in your device settings). That processing is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Apple gives us only aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

If you email us

If you write to us for support, we use only what you send (your email address, name and message) to answer you. We do not sell or share it. We delete it within a reasonable time after the conversation ends, and you can ask us to delete it sooner. Emails pass through and are stored by our email provider, whose servers may be outside your country.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Law No. 6698 (KVKK), California law and similar laws, you can ask whether we hold personal data about you, and ask to access, correct or delete it. Because the game collects no data, in practice this only applies to emails you send us. Write to the address below. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
